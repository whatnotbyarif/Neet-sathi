Let's understand photosynthesis

Short answer

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use sunlight to make food from carbon dioxide and water.

Step-by-step explanation

1. Absorb light: Chlorophyll in the leaves captures sunlight.

2. Take in raw materials: Roots absorb water, and leaves take in carbon dioxide.

3. Make glucose: Light energy helps convert these materials into food.

Photosynthesis at a glance
Key equationCarbon dioxide + Water + Light → Glucose + Oxygen
Real-life exampleA plant near a sunny window can make food as it receives light.
Remember: Chlorophyll captures light energy. Oxygen is released during photosynthesis.
